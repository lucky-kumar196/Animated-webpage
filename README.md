# SRJ Websto – Creative Digital Agency Website Template

A modern, creative, and visually engaging digital agency website template developed and presented by **SRJ Websto**.

This template is designed for creative agencies, web development companies, digital marketing agencies, branding studios, and freelance professionals.

## Template Overview

The website features a professional agency-style layout with creative visuals, interactive sections, project showcases, and modern design elements.

It helps businesses present their brand identity, showcase completed projects, highlight services, introduce their team, and connect with potential clients.

## Template Features

- Modern and creative homepage design
- Multiple homepage layout variations
- Creative agency and digital agency layouts
- Personal portfolio and freelancer layouts
- Interactive portfolio and project showcases
- Parallax scrolling effects
- Carousel and slider layouts
- Animated project cards and galleries
- Branding and design service sections
- Website and digital design showcase
- Team member section
- Client testimonials
- Awards and recognition section
- Creative approach and workflow section
- Blog and latest articles section
- Contact and project enquiry section
- Newsletter subscription section
- Responsive navigation
- Modern visual presentation

## Website Sections

- **Home:** Creative introduction and agency overview.
- **Works / Portfolio:** Project galleries, cards, carousels, and project details.
- **About Agency:** Agency introduction and creative vision.
- **Services:** Branding, website design, development, strategy, and marketing.
- **Our Team:** Team introduction and member profiles.
- **Testimonials:** Client feedback and reviews.
- **Our Approach:** Creativity, collaboration, strategy, and communication.
- **Awards:** Recognition and achievements showcase.
- **Blog:** Creative articles and latest updates.
- **Contact:** Project enquiries and business communication.
